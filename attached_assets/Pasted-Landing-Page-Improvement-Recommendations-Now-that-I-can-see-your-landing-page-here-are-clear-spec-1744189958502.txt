Landing Page Improvement Recommendations
Now that I can see your landing page, here are clear, specific improvements to increase its effectiveness:

Headline and Value Proposition
Add a specific number to your headline: "Small Business Owners: Save Up To 70% On Communication Costs"

Include a stronger subheadline that addresses a specific pain point: "While your competitors struggle with unreliable service and hidden fees, you'll enjoy premium communication at a fraction of the cost"

Visual Hierarchy
Create more contrast between the blue background and the white icons - they're currently hard to distinguish

Add a real image of a satisfied business owner or a before/after cost comparison chart

Use a two-tone design for your CTA button to make it pop more (orange with a subtle gradient)

Content Enhancements
Replace generic descriptions with specific benefits:

Instead of "Unlimited data with mobile hotspot capability," try "Never worry about data caps again + included mobile hotspot keeps you connected anywhere"

For "Special pricing for business owners," include an actual percentage or dollar amount

Trust Elements
Add a section with 2-3 customer testimonials with photos and business names

Include "As featured in" logos of relevant business publications

Add a money-back guarantee or "no risk trial" offer

Display industry certifications or security badges

Call-to-Action Improvements
Change CTA text to be more benefit-focused: "See My Custom Savings Plan →"

Add a small line of text under the button: "Free analysis, no obligation (takes just 2 minutes)"

Consider adding a secondary CTA for those not ready to calculate: "View Success Stories"

Layout Adjustments
Move the CTA button slightly higher on the page so it's visible without scrolling

Create more white space between elements to improve readability

Consider a short FAQ section addressing common objections

These specific changes will create a more compelling, conversion-focused landing page while maintaining your clean, professional aesthetic.
